# Why Dreema Exists

## The Topic

AI is improving fast—but it still runs on the same old backend. The part that matters most—the API, the data layer, the contract—hasn’t changed. So developers stay stuck reviewing inconsistent code and patching inconsistent responses, and AI can’t go full speed because there’s no single rule to follow. **Dreema puts you in absolute control while AI gets a clear target and runs faster.**

---

## The Real Problem

Backends are a free-for-all. Every endpoint returns something different: `{ "result": [...] }` here, `{ "items": [...] }` there, a raw array elsewhere. Errors are strings, objects, or framework-specific shapes. **You** have to remember the rules for each route. **You** debug by learning each endpoint’s local contract. When AI writes code, **you** can’t quickly tell if it fits—no fixed place for routes, logic, or data. Switch databases and **you** (and any AI helping you) rewrite the layer and learn a new API. Unpredictable responses and scattered structure slow everyone down: developers lose control of the contract, and AI has nothing consistent to aim at.

---

## What Dreema Does

Dreema puts **you** in control with one contract and one structure.

You define the rule: every response is **status**, **message**, **data**, **trace** when needed. One way to know success or failure. You know where everything lives—routes, controllers, models—so you review and correct quickly. One ORM across supported databases: you change **.env**, not the code. Because the backend is consistent, AI that calls your API parses once; AI that generates code has one pattern to follow. **You stay in control. AI runs faster.**

---

**Dreema is the backend that puts Human in control and AI gets blazingly fast in coding.**
